# Chamber Cardio

> Chamber Cardio is a Washington, D.C.-based healthcare technology company building a cardiology-focused platform for value-based care. It aligns cardiology signals, workflows, and patient data so cardiologists can identify high-risk patients earlier, close care gaps against treatment guidelines, and reduce administrative burden. Chamber supports 500+ cardiologists across seven states. Chamber does not currently publish a public developer API or documentation portal; its platform is built on a FHIR-based data stack (Medplum) with internal REST and GraphQL services.

## Notes

- No public developer API, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI spec, SDK, or developer portal has been published as of 2026-07-18.
- Technology context (per the published Medplum case study): FHIR-based data platform on Medplum, Retool front-ends via BonFHIR, Node.js/AWS Lambda services, REST + GraphQL.
- Funding: $60M Series A (Feb 2026) led by Frist Cressey Ventures; investors include General Catalyst, AlleyCorp, American Family Ventures, Company Ventures, Optum Ventures, Healthworx Ventures, Black Opal Ventures.
